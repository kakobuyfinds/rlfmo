What to check before ordering

  • Match the measurements

    For shoes, measure both feet and use the longer measurement with the seller’s chart. For tops, compare chest width and body length with a garment that fits you.

  • Check the selected variant

    Confirm the color, size and model on the source listing. If inspection photos are available, compare the labels, stitching and visible condition with the option you chose.

  • Estimate the delivered cost

    Add the selected item price, any domestic delivery and service fees, and international shipping. Check whether taxes or duties may apply at your destination.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the HipoBuy spreadsheet listings come from?

This independent guide presents a selection from the public HipobuyFind catalog. Each product name opens its corresponding listing on HipobuyFind.

Which products does the search cover?

Search checks product names and item numbers in the selection above. Use the HipoBuy spreadsheet button to browse the wider HipobuyFind catalog.

Why might the listing show a different price?

Sellers can change prices, and sizes or colors may carry different prices. Check the amount for your selected variant before ordering.
